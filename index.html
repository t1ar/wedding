How we met

It was race day, and Arron’s car refused to start on the grid with four minutes to go. Abby, a volunteer marshal in a bright orange vest, walked over to wave him off the track and ended up holding the torch while he fixed it.

He made the start, finished dead last and couldn’t stop smiling. She found him afterwards in the paddock to ask what was so funny. He said he’d been meaning to ask for her number, and she said the marshals’ radio was the only number she gave out.

Five years, a lot of lap times and one very patient cat later, we’re getting married. Watch our story, then come celebrate with us.
